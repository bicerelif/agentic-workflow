Email Router Product Specification

Overview
The Email Router is a workflow service that ingests inbound support emails, classifies them by intent, priority, and customer tier, then routes them to the correct support queue or automation path.

Goals
- Reduce manual triage time.
- Improve routing consistency.
- Provide visibility into routing decisions and failures.

Requirements
- Ingest emails from a shared mailbox or API endpoint.
- Normalize subject, sender, body, and attachment metadata.
- Classify each message into categories such as billing, technical issue, account access, and sales inquiry.
- Determine priority based on keywords, customer tier, and SLA impact.
- Route the message to a queue, human agent, or auto-response workflow.
- Log each decision with traceability and timestamps.
- Expose metrics for throughput, latency, routing accuracy, and error rate.
- Support retries and dead-letter handling for failed messages.

Constraints
- Must be secure and auditable.
- Must support incremental rollout behind a feature flag.
- Must allow future extension to additional channels.

Risks
- Misclassification could delay customer response.
- Incomplete metadata may reduce routing accuracy.
- Excessive automation without review may increase support burden.
