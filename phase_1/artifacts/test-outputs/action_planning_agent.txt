Sure! Here are the steps to make scrambled eggs:
Crack the desired number of eggs into a bowl.
Add a splash of milk or cream to the eggs (optional).
Season with salt and pepper to taste.
Beat the eggs with a fork or whisk until well combined.
Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
Add a small amount of butter or oil to the skillet.
Pour the beaten eggs into the skillet.
Let the eggs cook for a few seconds without stirring.
Use a spatula to gently stir and fold the eggs as they begin to set.
Continue to cook and stir the eggs until they reach your desired level of doneness.
Remove the scrambled eggs from the skillet and serve hot.
Enjoy your scrambled eggs!
